Hi! I am Jae Hyeon Kim, currently working as an embedded software engineer while pursuing a Master’s degree in Intelligent Software at Ajou University.

My professional background includes embedded Linux system development, such as Yocto-based platforms, Linux kernel and bootloader (U-Boot) customization, and board bring-up on SoC-based hardware.
As part of my professional work, I have also been involved in NPU-based AI workflows, including AI model conversion, optimization, and validation on embedded devices.
Through this experience, I became interested in AI for edge devices, particularly NPU-enabled inference under hardware constraints.

My current academic and research interests focus on Edge AI, including AI model compilation, model optimization and compression and efficient deployment of deep learning models on embedded systems.
